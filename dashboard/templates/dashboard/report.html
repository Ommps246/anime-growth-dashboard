4 · Method

Four CSV files are loaded into SQLite through Django models. pandas reads the tables back and does the grouping, cross-tabulation and share calculations; NumPy handles the growth-rate and error-band arithmetic. Matplotlib draws the static figures and the PDF. scikit-learn fits the trend models. The interactive charts fetch the same results as JSON from the dashboard's own API.

5 · Limits

Title counts depend on what fan-maintained catalogues have recorded, and small or older works are under-represented. Manga is measured in titles launched, not sales, and the data ends in 2022. Market figures before 2008 exclude pachinko and before 2013 exclude live events, because AJA did not survey them. The forecasts are trend extensions, not causal models.
